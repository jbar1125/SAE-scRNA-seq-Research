# Suggested PR title

Audit gene-program interpretation with completed CPU experiments and publication review drafts

# Description

Biological annotations on learned features did not reliably determine their
structure or perturbation responses. This branch preserves the failed structural
hypothesis, fixes an unmatched-feature index bug, audits modulation-test limits,
and adds completed Norman CPU experiments, complete comparison families and a
manuscript with PDF/Word review copies. Frozen specifications and both Gate-0
bundles remain unchanged. Historical causal JSONs were lost; their old numbers
are excluded from the primary manuscript.

## Findings

- **Structural hypothesis unsupported:** 0/10 SAE seeds support the original
  direction in each dataset; only mouse NMF supports it among five methods and
  two datasets. Marker omissions do not change those verdicts.
- **Inference limits exposed:** legacy quantitative gates accept identical
  generic responses in 20/20 synthetic worlds. Conditional identity inference
  still rejects all nine panels in 100/100 variable-strength generic worlds.
  Invalid clustered-cell shuffling rejects in 100/100 simulations versus 8/100
  for a separate cluster-mean analysis. Complete outputs and uncertainty are
  retained; these are simulations, not biological validation.
- **Full-size fits completed:** all three 2,048-feature primary SAE seeds and
  one control-only sensitivity finished 200 epochs. With the original nine
  panels, no candidate combines the legacy background gate and agreement in at
  least two seeds. Four complete model records and actual input, checkpoint and
  activation hashes are preserved.
- **A historical program gives positive conditional recovery:** the exact
  previously selected eight-gene K562 signature yields 63/61/61 all-cell SAE
  associations, 57 direct-score calls, 65 PCA calls, 54/52 capped/warm NMF calls
  and 8 control-only SAE calls. All 8,400 associations and 80 identity hypotheses
  enter correction. Thirty-eight direct-score calls survive all four negative
  references, depth matching and every single-gene omission, including CBL,
  UBASH3B and PTPN12. These three meet the criterion for all eight individual
  transcripts. KLF1 meets seven; GYPB AUC .519 becomes .521 after excluding
  signature genes from the count denominator. All unfavorable outcomes remain.
  Historical selection used the same data, so nominal p/q values do not calibrate
  the full selection process. Context and program granularity changed together.
- **Numerical stopping changes annotation:** the bounded NMF warm restart meets
  tolerance after 380 further iterations. Reconstruction error decreases 0.88%,
  and a membrane annotation appears. Both capped and warm fits are retained in
  complete 6,615-association/63-identity families. This is not an uninterrupted
  fit, a global optimum or a biological method ranking.
- **Shared labels measure different responses:** common six-model inference
  retains 5,670 associations and 54 identity tests. PCA alone has 52 Cycling-up
  calls; 20 have both external phase AUCs at most .45. Verified signed-weight
  attribution preserves all 428,505 gene contributions and explains mean-score
  direction, without explaining rank effects or cellular mechanism.
- **Additional controls retained:** all 109 direct/alternative target-RNA
  comparisons, all 1,179 dual-marker contrasts and the complete joint 2,358-test
  log/linear sensitivity remain archived. Technical partitions and computation
  seeds are not biological replicates. Known Norman examples are not novel
  regulator discoveries.

## Review and verification

The manuscript source, PDF and editable Word copy include four main figures and
one supplementary figure. The claim-to-evidence ledger, reproduction commands,
full result records, ten checked citation records and targeted literature review
are versioned. The original Norman abstract and selected author code were read;
its full article and supplements remain unchecked.

Twenty-four distinct standalone suites passed: fifteen together and nine
separately. Syntax, actual full-data research executions, score recovery,
complete-family corrections, frozen hashes and deterministic figure checks pass.
The review renderer verifies the source/figure hashes, PDF content and five
embedded Word figures. See `docs/CONTINUATION_VERIFICATION.md` for exact scopes.

Seven verified large archives total 1,696,336,750 bytes locally. The GitHub release
is an empty draft because the proxy denies CONNECT to `uploads.github.com`
before authentication. The host is saved in the environment configuration draft;
publishing that setting is needed before retrying. No public deposition is claimed.

The results establish conditional program associations and representation and
annotation dependence in these systems. They establish no novel regulator
mechanism, independent biological replication or SAE advantage. Author facts,
contributions/disclosures, primary-text review and durable large-artifact
deposition remain unresolved. This remains a draft PR, with no submission or
author approval implied.
